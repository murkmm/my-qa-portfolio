**Company:** Unity

### Getting Fall Guys onto phones

At Unity, I managed the testing workflow for the mobile version of Fall Guys. That meant reviewing requirements, planning coverage, testing builds and reporting on whether they were ready for release.

### Different phones, different problems

The physics and movement needed to hold up across a wide range of devices. I tested different screen sizes, operating system versions and hardware, with checks for memory use, battery consumption and input responsiveness.

I put the findings into QA reports and dashboards so the people making the release decision could see the outstanding issues and risks.
