**Company:** T.T. Games

### Lots of characters, lots to test

I worked on Year 2 DLC for LEGO Dimensions, including Story Packs, Level Packs and the multiplayer Battle Arenas. The packs covered Fantastic Beasts, Ghostbusters, The LEGO Batman Movie, Sonic, Adventure Time and Mission: Impossible.

The toys-to-life setup meant checking combinations of characters, vehicles and gadgets across PS3, PS4, Xbox 360, Xbox One and Wii U. I tested abilities, progression and UI, reported bugs in Jira and worked through regression lists to check fixes.

### The characters walking into walls

I logged more than 200 Battle Arena bugs. One that stuck with me involved AI characters: some of the less popular ones had broken pathing and would walk into walls. Testing beyond the obvious main characters was what exposed it.

I put together a test plan covering every AI-controlled character afterwards. I also tested the main story and level content, from visual glitches through to bugs that stopped progression. I quite liked clearing the long regression lists, which probably says something about why I ended up in QA.
